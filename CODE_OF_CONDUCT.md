# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and harassment-free project environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, evidence-based, and constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, authorship, and licensing.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about defects and risks.
- Accept correction and acknowledge mistakes.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or intimidation.
- Insults, personal attacks, sexualized content, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately uploading malicious, confidential, stolen, or unlawfully distributed files.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or bad-faith misrepresentation of test results.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile contact method. Include relevant links or screenshots and avoid further public escalation while the report is being reviewed.

Maintainers should evaluate reports fairly, preserve confidentiality where practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and apply proportionate consequences.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public interactions where a person is representing the project.
