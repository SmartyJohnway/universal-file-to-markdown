   When top decoding candidates for Big5, CP950, GB18030 score within close confidence margin, system reports `encoding_status: "ambiguous"`, sets `explicit_encoding_recommended: true`, and emits `ENCODING_AMBIGUOUS` warning.    Created native text converter (`convert_text.py`) for `.txt`, `.text`, `.log` and native Markdown converter (`convert_markdown.py`) for `.md`, `.markdown`. Evaluated natively in `router.py` without requiring Pandoc or MarkItDown.
